# choc cobbler

## Ingredients
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 80g flour
- 1tsp baking powder
- pinch of salt
- 100g white sugar
- 1 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 135g white sugar
- 1/6 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cups boiling water

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Melt butter in an 8x8 inch baking dish while the oven preheats.
2. In a medium bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, 100g sugar, and 1 tablespoons cocoa.
   Stir in milk and vanilla until smooth. Spoon this batter over the melted butter in the baking dish.
3. Stir together the remaining 135g of sugar and 1/6 cup cocoa powder. Sprinkle over the batter.
   Slowly pour boiling water over the top of the mixture.
4. Bake for 30 minutes in the preheated oven, until set.
