# Banana (with carrot) Bread

banana bread with crushed candied walnuts, candied shredded carrot, topped with chopped pecans

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 TBS hemp hearts
- 2TBS wheat germ
- 1/2 cup soft butter
- 3/4 cup sugar & molasses +1/4-1/2 cup sugar for nuts
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 tsp vanilla/some vanilla bean
- 1 tbsp milk/milk powder
- 1 cup chopped walnuts
- 1 cup shredded carrots
- chopped pecans

## Steps
1. Candied walnuts
   1 cup chopped walnuts
   2-3 TBS butter
   1/4-1/2 cup white sugar
   dash salt
   some water
   cook low-med heat for a few minutes, lay out on parchment
2. Candied shredded carrots
   1-3 TBS butter
   1/4-1/2 Cup white sugar
   dash salt
   some molasses
   some water
   (forgot to add) dash cinnamon
3. Whisk flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, hemp hearts, & wheat germ, set aside
   Mash bananas, set aside
   Cream soft butter with sugar using spatula
   Add banana mash to butter mix and whisk until combined
   Add eggs, 1 at a time
   Add vanilla & milk
   Add candied carrots & candied walnuts
   Add flour, mix with spatula gently, lightly folding
4. Spray loaf pan(s)
   Fill (used small loaf trays) 1/2-3/4 of the way
   Sprinkle with chopped pecans
5. Bake at 350F for 15 minutes
   Lower temperature to 325F for about 10 minutes or until center of bread reads 200F
