# Biscuits

## Ingredients
### Parts:
- 1) tangzhong mix
- 2) biscuit mix
- 3) buttermilk biscuit top-wash
### 1) Tangzhong Mix:
- 2 tablespoons bread flour
- 2 tablespoons water
- 4 tablespoons whole milk (or: 1 1/3 tbsp of DRY milk + 8 tablespoons of water)
### 2) Biscuit Mix:
- 1 cup of all purpose flour
- 1 cup of bread flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 package of instant rapid rise yeast
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 5Tbsp of unsalted butter, VERY cold
- 1/4 cup of DRY cultured buttermilk
- 1 cup of water
- cooked, room temperature tangzhong
### 3) buttermilk biscuit top-wash:
- butter (1tbsp) and buttermilk (from dry, totaling about 1/3 cup) for brushing top of biscuits before placing into oven

## Steps
### Instructions:
1. •Empty the ingredients for Tangzhong Mix into a frying pan.
2. •Cook, stirring constantly until looks like sticky mashed potatoes (about 1min@ medium heat).
3. •Let cooked tangzhong sit to room temperature
4. •Meanwhile in a separate bowl, combining the dry ingredients of the Biscuit Mix.
5. •Add cooked and cool tangzhong to dry biscuit mix, along with five tablespoons of cut unsalted butter, and 1 cup of cool water.
6. •Sit to rise at leas 1-2 hours after lightly folding
7. •Mix gently, basic incorporation. Do not over knead or overmix.
8. •Put mix on floured surface, gently fold into layers for about 5 minutes
9. •Cut into 3” biscuits about 1/2” to 1” desired thickness (1” preferable).
10. •Let rise 1 hour
11. •After biscuits are cut, let rise at room temperature for one hour, then chill for at least 15 minutes.
12. •For best biscuits -- prior to folding, cutting and rise; let dough rise in refrigerator for at least 4 days.
13. •Cover dough with 2-3Tbsp melted butter before refrigeration and keep dough airtight.
14. •Brush tops with buttermilk top-wash before putting in oven.
15. •Bake @ 400F for around 5-10 minutes until tops are golden crispy
