# Glazed Donuts

## Ingredients
### •500g flour; HALF recipe on right:
- •167g All Purpose flour 83.5g
- •167g Cake flour 83.5g
- •167g Bread flour 83.5g
- •7g fine salt 3.5g
- •3g amylase 1.5-2g
- •2tsp Rapid Rise Yeast 1tsp
- •300g/ml milk (or; 150g-180g cream +130g-150g water) 150g (75g &75g)
- •35g-85g granulated sugar 17.5g-45g LESS
- •1 egg 1/2 egg
- •2Tbs shortening/crisco 1TBS
- •2Tbs unsalted butter 1TBS
- •1/3 vanilla bean + splash vanilla (bean not necessary, but worth it)
- •Crisco for frying
- GLAZE half recipe on right
- •~400g powdered sugar 200g
- • pinch of salt 1/2 pinch
- • 20g corn syrup 10g
- • ~140g cream (warmed with vanilla bean slightly) 70g
- • 1/3 - 2/3 vanilla bean sm splash warmed up vanilla
- best refrigerated and then whisked with more cream

## Steps
1. •Weigh out flours & salt, amylase combine, set aside
2. •In a Large bowl, measure out yeast
3. •In a Smaller saucepan (or microwave bowl), weigh out milk(cream+water) & sugar, combine
4. •Medium heat, warm milk sugar mixture until 115F (microwave, probably 20 sec? intervals)
5. •Do not exceed 140F or it will kill yeast
6. •Pour milk mixture into yeast, whisk
7. •Let mix sit about 15 minutes
8. •Melt butter and Crisco together, cool
9. •Add vanilla, egg, + melted butter & Crisco to milk yeast mix, whisk
10. •Add flour mix to milk mix, combine (I use silicone spatula)
11. •On a very lightly floured surface, gently knead/fold dough for 5-7 minutes
12. •Grease large bowl with Crisco
13. •Make dough into ball and place into well greased bowl
14. •Cover and let rise 2-3 hours
15. •After, either roll out and shape dough or rest in fridge overnight
16. •Press dough down
17. •Roll dough out into about 1/8" to 1/4"
18. •With an uncrustable sandwich cutter (any proper sized circular cutter), cut out donuts
19. •Cut out center of donut with medicine cup bottom or proper sized equivalent
20. •Once cut, let rise 30 minutes to 1 hour on a foiled, lightly floured, sheet pan
21. •Prepare wire racks (and sheet trays to lay under them, foiled/covered)
22. •Once risen, in a Large pan or wok (I use a wok, on a plug in heating element for less mess) heat Crisco to 350F, no more or donuts will burn
23. •Fill wok a little less than halfway
24. •With a candy thermometer, check temperature of Crisco (don't touch bottom of pan)
25. •As soon as temperature says around 345F and climbing, quickly but carefully place as many donuts as you can fit into the hot Crisco
26. •Set a 1 minute timer as soon as you put the last donut of the batch in
27. •Keep and eye on donuts as not to burn
28. •Flip after 1 minute and cook 1 more minute
29. •Cook 1 minute on each side
30. •Donuts will be a light to medium brown/golden
31. •With a spider strainer, strain out donuts onto wire rack
32. •Continue process until all dough is fried
33. •While donuts are still warm, dip in glaze on either side, tapping gently on side of bowl to remove excess
34. •Place donuts back onto wire racks to let glaze set
35. •Continue until all donuts are glazed and glaze is set
36. Enjoy~
