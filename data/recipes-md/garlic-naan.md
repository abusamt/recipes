# Garlic Naan

Chainbaker recipe

## Ingredients
- For the dough –
- 250g (8.8oz) strong white bread flour
- 5g (0.2oz) salt
- 5g (0.2oz) sugar
- 3-6g (0.1oz) dry yeast or three times the amount of fresh yeast
- 10g (0.35oz) oil
- 60g (2.1oz) yogurt at around 20C (68F)
- 100g (3.5oz) milk at around 20C (68F)
- For the garlic butter –
- 2 tablespoons of softened butter
- Chopped parsley
- 1 minced clove of garlic
- Pinch of salt (Mix everything until combined ready for spreading)

## Steps
1. Add the milk and yeast to a bowl and let the yeast hydrate for a couple of minutes.
2. Add the salt, sugar, oil and yogurt and give it a good mix to dissolve any salt and sugar crystals.
3. Add the flour and mix until combined.
4. Knead the dough for around 5 minutes. Desired dough temperature 25 – 26C (77 – 79F). If your dough is
   warmer, then reduce the proofing time. If it is cooler, then increase proofing time.
5. Ferment for 25 minutes. My kitchen as quite warm on the day of baking, so I had to cut down on fermentation time. Keep an eye on your dough and if it is fermenting rapidly, react.
6. Fold.
7. Proof for 25 minutes.
8. Divide into 4 equal pieces and shape into balls.
9. Cover and proof for around 30 minutes.
10. Roll the dough out and rub with oil.
11. Cook in a pan on medium-high heat flipping every 30 seconds for around 4 minutes or until well coloured and puffed up. You can use more oil to get a more evenly brown colour.
12. Brush the breads with the garlic butter and enjoy whilst still warm with your favourite curry.
