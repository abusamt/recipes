# Thumbprint Cookies

## Ingredients
- 1 stick (1/2 cup) soft unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup powdered sugar (not sifted)
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour (5 ounces by weight)
- 1/2 cup fruit jam to fill centers
- 325 F. for 15 minutes

## Steps
1. Combine powdered sugar with a little milk or water, and mix until you have a consistency that will hold its shape when piped.
