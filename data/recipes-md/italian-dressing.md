# Italian Dressing

Half recipe on RIGHT

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup olive oil 1/4 cup
- 3 Tbsp white wine vinegar 1.5 Tbs
- 3 Tbsp grated parmesan cheese 1.5 Tbs
- 1 tsp dijon mustard 1/2 tsp
- 1 tsp honey 1/2 tsp
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (1/2 tsp powder) -- 1/4 tsp
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano 1/4 tsp
- 1/2 tsp dried basil 1/4 tsp
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley 1/4 tsp
- 1/2 tsp salt 1/4 tsp
- 1/2 tsp black pepper 1/4 tsp

## Steps
1. In a medium bowl or jar, whisk together all ingredients until smooth and creamy. Serve immediately or store in an airtight container in the fridge until ready to serve.
