# cucumber avocado salad

## Ingredients
- - 1 normal cucumber or 2 smaller ones
- - 2 smaller avocados
- - 1/4 to 1/2 a carrot, thinly julienned
- - 2 TBS or so of lemon juice
- - salt & pepper
- - 2 scallions
- red pepper flakes, everything bagel seasoning

## Steps
1. sliced thinly the scallions-- add to ice water
   peel and chop cucumber, place in bowl with salt 5-15 minutes
   drain cucumbers
   chop avocado, mix with lemon juice, salt & pepper, & julienned carrots
   drain scallions
   add all together and stir
   add everything bagel seasoning and red pepper flakes
