# Wonton soup

## Ingredients
- 1 LB ground chicken
- 6-10 cups chicken broth (used bouillon)
- 1 pkg shiitake mushrooms
- 1 bok choy
- 1 bunch green onion
- 2 ish TBS minced ginger
- 2-4 cloves minced garlic
- 1 TBS sherry vinegar
- 1/4 tsp sesame oil
- 1TBS soy sauce
- chili crisp oil
- kombu
- umami mushroom powder
- sesame seeds
- wonton wrappers
- 2 block of tofu for topping
- marinated in hoisin and soy sauce (about 1-2 TBS each)
- homemade wontons: recipe is 1.5x
- 375g / bread flour or half ap and bread flour
- ~200 ml / water
- pinch salt

## Steps
1. WONTONS
   mix ingredients
   rest/can rest in fridge up to whole day or so
   use pasta roller to get thin enough
   cut into squares
2. MARINATE TOFU OVERNIGHT FIRST!
3. brown mushrooms
4. mix ground chicken, 3 finely minced/chopped green onions, soy sauce, sesame oil, sesame seeds, some chili oil, garlic, ginger, sherry vinegar, little bit of broth,shake of umami mushroom powder
### kombu stock:
5. 3 cup of cold water, 1 sheet of kombu. sit for 10 minutes.
   heat gently to 140F, turn off heat and set for 2 minutes, then extract kombu
### stock:
6. browned shitake @ 4
   (kombu stock made with 3cup water)
   1 tbsp of chili crisp
7. add more water, add chopped whites of bok choy & rest of green onions
   simmer for a while
8. add green parts of bok choy
9. wrap chicken mixture in wonton wrappers while broth simmers
   bring broth to boil and add wontons
10. Cook 3 MINUTES
