# oil gummies (pectin and gelatin)

## Ingredients
- 160g coconut oil
- 150g sugar + 100g water + 100g corn syrup
- 35g gelatin + 160g juice
- 1TBSP lecithin
- 10g pectin
- 1/3tsp potassium sorbate
- 100g gelatin
- 1 dram bottle flavouring oil

## Steps
1. bloom 160g juice with 35g gelatin
2. boil to 220-250F: 150g sugar + 90g water + 100g corn syrup
3. let syrup coil to 200F
4. add 10g of pectin, 1/3tsp potassium sorbate, stir out clumps
5. add 100g of gelatin, stirring out clumps
6. add bloomed gelatin, lecithin
7. add oil
8. add flavouring oil
9. whisk on low heat, consistently, until mixed and emulsified. fold edges into center as stirring
