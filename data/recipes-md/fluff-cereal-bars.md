# fluff cereal bars

## Ingredients
- 1.5 Cups plain cheerios
- .5 Cup Bran flakes
- .5 Cup Maple Magic Spoon
- .5 Cup granola crushed some
- .5 Cup toasted almond slivers
- 1 Tbs toasted wheat germ
- 1-2 Tbs caramel packet from brownie mix
- 1 Cup fluff
- 4 Tbs butter (used 1 TBS butter, 1.5 Tbs avocado oil, 1.5 Tbs butter shortening) use less
- 1 scoop protein powder
- pinch salt in toasted almonds

## Steps
1. Mix together all dry ingredients (toasted almonds, toasted wheat germ, bran flakes, cheerios, magic spoon, granola)
   melt together butter, add fluff to butter, caramel, scoop of protein powder, mix until combined
   once combined, pour over dry
   mix until evenly coated
   press into greased bar tray/pan
   sit 30 minutes in fridge
