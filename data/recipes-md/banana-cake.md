# Banana Cake

Recipe by (Betty) Lynette Meeks

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup butter/shortening (half and half)
- 2.5 cups sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1.5 cups mashed bananas
- 3 cups sifted flour (half cake half all purpose)
- 3/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/3 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1.5 tsp vanilla
- For Frosting
- 1.5 box (cups?) (~ half a bag) (bag = 2lb or 907g) powdered sugar
- 3/4 cup (1.5 sticks) butter
- 3/4 cup mashed bananas
- 1.5 tsp lemon juice

## Steps
1. Cream together shortening, butter, and sugar, blending well.
   Add well beaten eggs and bananas, beat well
   Sift flours then resift with dry ingredients
   Add dry alternatively with wet mixture of buttermilk and vanilla combined
2. In greased, floured pans bake at 350F for about 25 minutes or until done
   Remove from pan and cool
3. For Frosting
   Cream all ingredients together
   Spread onto cooled cake
