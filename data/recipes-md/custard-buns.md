# Custard Buns

## Ingredients
### INGREDIENTS:
- Preparing the custard
- 500ml (2 cups) Milk
- 2 Eggs
- 100g (1/2 cup) Sugar
- 30g (3 1/2 tbsp) Cornstarch
- A pinch of salt
- 2 tsp Vanilla
- Prepare the dough
- 200ml (1 cup) Warm Milk
- 1 tbsp Yeast
- 100g (1/2 cup) Sugar
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 2 Eggs
- 90g (1 cup) Butter (soften at room temperature)
- 600g (about 4-5 cups) Plain flour
- Topping
- 1 Egg yolk + 3 tbsp Milk
- Almond flaked
- -------HALF RECIPE--------
- 250ml (1 cup) milk
- 1 egg
- 50g (1/4 cup) sugar
- 15g (1.75 Tbsp) cornstarch
- 1/2 pinch salt
- 1 tsp vanilla
- DOUGH
- 100ml (1/2 cup) milk warm
- 1/2 TBS yeast
- 50g (1/4 cup) sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 1 egg
- 45g (1/2 cup) softened butter
- 300g (2-2.5 cups) flour
- topping
- 1 egg yolk & 3 Tbs milk
- almond flaked
### for chocolate:
- add to the custard 2 Tbs cocoa powder
- add to the dough 1/2 cup (50g) cocoa and 1/2 cup (100g) sugar

## Steps
1. Add milk to saucepan
   add egg, sugar, salt, cornstarch (also some vanillin) and vanilla bean
   Heat on MED heat for about 5 minute while continuously stirring
   once mixture thickens, place into bowl and cover with plastic wrap, set aside
2. for dough
   add warm milk and yeast to bowl, whisk, add sugar, egg, vanilla- whisk
   add softened butter, whisk
   add flour slowly,
   mix until combined
   knead about 5 minutes or until basically smooth and not sticky
   let sit out for an hour
   either place in fridge or use immediately (sitting in fridge is easier to handle)
   roll out dough pretty thin, 1/2 to 1/4 inch?
   spread on custard
   roll up
   cut thin!! with floss
   set on tray and cover with plastic for ~ 15 minutes, egg wash and add almonds if adding
   then baked in air fryer @ ~335F for 8-10 minutes +
