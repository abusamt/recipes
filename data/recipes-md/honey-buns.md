# Honey Buns

## Ingredients
### Honey Buns:
- • 2 cups all-purpose flour
- • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- • 1⁄2 teaspoon salt
- • 1⁄2 cup + 3 tablespoons milk (WATER + CREAM)
- • 2 1⁄4 teaspoons (1 packet) instant or active dry yeast
- • 5 1⁄3 tablespoons (1⁄3 cup) butter melted (HALF CRISCO)
- • 1 large egg
- • 1⁄2 tablespoon vegetable oil
- • 1 1⁄2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- • 3 cups vegetable oil for frying (optional)
### Glaze:
- • 3 tablespoons butter melted
- • 2 teaspoon honey
- • 11⁄4 cups powdered sugar
- • 2 tablespoons milk
- • 1⁄2 teaspoon vanilla extract or bean equivalent

## Steps
### Honey Buns:
1. • Whisk together flour, sugar, & salt
2. • In a microwave-safe bowl, add in the milk. Microwave for 30 seconds.
   (can use stovetop)
   • milk should be at 105 - 110 degrees F.
3. • Add yeast, cooled melted butter, and egg to milk
4. • Pour the wet mixture into dry & mix gently (using very little flour)
   for 10 minutes.
   ~The dough will be sticky.
5. • Grease your hands and a large bowl with vegetable oil.
6. • Shape dough into a ball.
7. • Place the dough in the bottom of the bowl and coat it in the 1/2 Tbs oil.
   • Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the dough rise until doubled.
   • This will take about 1 to 2 hours.
8. • Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper/foil.
   • Spray the parchment paper with nonstick baking spray.
9. • Once the dough has doubled, press it down.
10. • Place the dough on a lightly floured work surface.
11. • Press the dough out into a 6-inch by 14-inch rectangle.
   ~You can use a rolling pin, or use your hands.
12. • Brush honey onto rectangular dough (thin layer)
   • Sprinkle the cinnamon on top of the honey & dough.
   • Using your brush (or hands), rub the cinnamon all over
13. • Begin rolling the dough from the long side.
14. • Once the dough is rolled up, you'll need to seal the edge.
   • Do this by rubbing the edge of the dough with water.
   • Pinch the wet edge into the roll of dough to form the seam.
   • If the dough isn't staying pinched together, rub a little more water on the edge and pinch again.
   • Leave the dough seam side up since it will be sticky from the water and will likely stick to the counter if it touches it.
15. • Measure out 1⁄2-inch thick slices if you want thinner honey buns or 1-inch slice for thicker slices.
   (1-inch or thicker)
16. • Slide a piece of floss under the dough and cross both sides of the floss
   • Pull until the floss has cut completely through the dough.
17. • Place on the prepared cookie sheet.
   • Repeat with the remaining dough.
18. If you plan on baking these, you'll want to space them 2 inches apart.
   If you plan on frying them, they can be 1⁄2 inch apart.
19. • Brush entire surface of honey bun with honey
20. • Let rise, lightly covered, for 30 minutes in a warm place.
### --Baked Honey Buns:
21. • Preheat oven to 350F.
22. -Bake for 10 to 15 minutes until the edges begin to turn golden brown-
23. • Prepare the glaze while the honey buns are baking.
24. • Remove the honey buns from the oven and let cool for 10 to 20 minutes.
   • While they're still warm, dip the tops in the glaze, or place icing on top and rub in with spoon.
   • Tap the wrist that's holding the honey bun to remove the excess glaze
   • Place on wire rack or wax to continue cooling & let the glaze set up.
### --Fried Honey Buns:
25. • Heat 3 cups of vegetable oil until it reaches 320F.
26. • Prepare the glaze while you wait for the oil to come up to temperature.
27. • Place the honey buns in one or two at a time into the oil.
   • Flip once the donuts become golden brown.
28. • Once the other side becomes golden brown, place on a plate lined with paper towels.
   • Let cool for 5 to 10 minutes.
29. • Dip the warm honey bun in the glaze.
   • Give your wrist a tap to help any excess glaze to drip off.
   • Place on a piece of wax paper and let the glaze set up.
### --Glaze:
30. • In a medium mixing bowl, add in the butter, powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla extract/bean.
   • Whisk together until the glaze is smooth.
