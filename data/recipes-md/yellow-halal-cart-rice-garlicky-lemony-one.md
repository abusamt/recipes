# yellow halal cart rice (garlicky lemony one)

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water
- 1 TBS bouiilon
- 1 tsp garlic powder or less
- 1/2 Tbs ghee
- pinch smoked paprika
- dash of MSG
- lemon juice

## Steps
1. mix water and spices and ghee, mix more when melted
   when done, squeeze lemon juice over rice and fluff together
