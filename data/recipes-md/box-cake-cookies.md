# Box Cake Cookies

This recipe makes cookies from any 15.25 oz (432 g) box of cake mix.

## Ingredients
- 1 box cake mix, 15.25 oz / 432 g
- 2 large eggs, approximately 100 g without shells
- 1/2 cup oil / approximately 120 g; avocado oil works well

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 335-350°F / 168-177°C.
2. Mix the cake mix, eggs, and oil until combined.
3. Scoop the dough onto a lined baking sheet. Cookie size does not matter too much.
4. Bake for about 8-10 minutes, turning the tray halfway through baking.
5. Cool the cookies before serving.
