# A Recipe for Brioche with Raisins--not yet tried/read

## Ingredients
### For the dough:
- 500g white flour.
- 50g butter.
- 1 egg.
- 60g sugar.
- 2 tbsp powder milk.
- 15g baking yeast.
- 1 baking powder.
### For the custard ( crème patissiére):
- 2 cups milk.
- 1 egg.
- 2 tbsp sugar.
- 1tsp vanilla.
- 20g flour or cornflower (cornstarch).
- 100g raisin for garnish.

## Steps
### Way of preparation:
### The dough:
1. In a big bowl, put the dry ingredients then add butter and mix well.
2. Add an egg and warm water little by little then knead well until the dough is smooth and cohesive.
3. Let the dough rest for an hour in the refrigerator. (It’s better to keep it in the fridge for the whole night)
4. You can use puff pastry.
### The custard:
5. Mix an egg with sugar, vanilla and cornflower.
6. In a saucepan, boil milk then add it to the first mixture and mix well.
7. Put the mixture on a low heat and keep stirring until we get a thick cream.
8. Put the cream in refrigerator.
9. Make two balls from the dough and flatten them with a rolling pin.
10. Spread custard on the surface and sprinkle raisins on the top then roll the dough.
11. Cut the rolling dough into circles (you can put the rolling dough in the freezer for an hour to keep its shape while cutting)
12. Place brioche in an oven tray and brash them with a whipped egg then sprinkle some almonds flakes or ground almonds on the top. (Optional)
13. Bake it in a pre heated oven for 25 to 30 minutes.
14. After baking you can brush the brioche with jam.
15. Serve with tea!
