# italian wedding soup with turkey meatballs

## Ingredients
- 10-12 cups chicken broth
- 2 Tbs olive oil
- 1 lb ground turkey lean
- 1/2 cup panko
- 1/3 cup parsley finely chopped
- 1 egg
- 3-4 minced garlic cloves
- 1 tsp salt kosher
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 cup parmesan
- 3/4 cup orzo
- 3 cups packed baby spinach
- 1 lemon, zested and halved
- 1 tsp black garlic added while simmering
- 1 TBS umami powder
- 1-2 cups? broiled carrot large chop diagonal
- 1-2 cups carrots cut largely broiled

## Steps
1. Step 1
   Combine broth with 2 TBS olive oil in large pot-- bring to boil
2. Step 2
   Add turkey, panko, parsley, egg, garlic, salt, pepper, & 1/2 cup parmesan to a large bowl- mix
   roll into 1&1/2 inch balls
   transfer to pan for broiling
3. Step 3
   Heat broiler to high
   set oven rack 6-8 inches away
   broil meatballs until browned on each side
4. Step 4
   Add pasta to boiling broth and cook until al-dente
   lower heat to low simmer
5. Step 5
   Add meatballs to broth and simmer 3-5 minutes until warmed through
   remove from heat
   add spinach and lemon zest and stir
   season to taste with salt and pepper
6. Step 6
   Divide soup among 4 bowls
   drizzle each with olive oil, parmesan, and a squeeze of lemon juice
