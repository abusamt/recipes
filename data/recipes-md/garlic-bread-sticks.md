# garlic bread sticks

## Ingredients
- 1 Tbs instant yeast
- 2 Tbs sugar
- 1 cup 100-110F water
- 2&1/2-3 cups flour; Half bread flour half all-purpose-- more bread than ap
- 2 tsp salt +?
- 3 Tbs olive oil
- for topping
- 4 Tbs melted butter
- 2 Tbs + parmesan
- 1 tsp mix herbs de Provence + Italian seasoning
- 2-3 cloves minced garlic
- some salt

## Steps
1. combine warm water, yeast, sugar, salt, + oil
   add 2 cups flour
   once incorporated, slowly add rest of flour
   dough pulls from side of bowl, not too wet
   transfer dough to a lightly greased bowl
   cover with damp towel or paper towel +foil 30 mins- 1 hour+ (until doubled)(longer better)
   line baking sheet with parchment + lightly spray
   punch down dough
   divide dough into 12 equal pieces (around 50g-60g)
   9-10 inch rope roll out
   cover with damp paper towel
   let rise 20 minutes - 1 hour on sheet pan once rolled out
   bake 425F 10-15 minutes
### for topping:
2. while baking, heat butter until just browned
   remove from heat
   add in garlic cloves, sit ~30 seconds, then strain garlic out
   pour butter into cup/glass with parmesan, dry herbs/spice mixture, salt
   use brush to immediately spread onto tops of bread sticks
