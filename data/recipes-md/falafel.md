# falafel

## Ingredients
- 2 cups dried chickpeas (soaked 12 hours)
- 1 L onion chopped or 2 medium/sm
- 1/4 cup parsley and cilantro (more)
- 4 sliced garlic cloves
- pinch baking powder?
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp cumin
- salt to taste (1-2 tsp)
- xanthan gum 2 tsp
- besan to coat

## Steps
1. Combine soaked chickpeas with onion, herbs and garlic in a large bowl, stir together
   Scoop into food processor
   Blend and hold, stir with spatula, scrape sides
   add little bit of water if needed
   Dump mixture resembling ricotta? into bowl
   Add spices, baking powder, and xanthan gum
   Mix thoroughly with spatula
2. shape into falafel
   coat with besan
   fry 350F oil?
   until golden and crisp

## Notes
- Need oven tray lined with parchment (for falafel balls)
- Need oven tray lined with parchment and oven rack (for cooked falafel)
