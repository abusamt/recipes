# Snickerdoodles with icing

## Ingredients
- 125g cake flour + 120g ap + 125g bread flour
- 7g cream of tartar
- 5g baking soda
- 2g baking powder - or a big pinch, half teaspoon
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 300g granulated sugar + molasses (try 200-250g)
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter at room temperature
- 1/2 cup butter flavored shortening
- 1 large egg
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- some cinnamon
### Cinnamon Sugar Topping:
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 3 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons turbinado sugar
- OVEN: 350
- TIME: 8-10 mins
- Icing
- ~250g powdered sugar
- ~1/2 vanillin sugar packet
- ~Tbs corn syrup
- -as much cream as needed
- -pinch of salt

## Steps
1. combine dry ingredients + cinnamon
   whisk
   combine (mixer) sugar with butter + molasses
   add egg + egg yolk
   add vanilla
   add dry to wet and mix until combined
   chill in fridge
   roll into balls
   coat with topping
   bake ~10 minutes
   cool
   icing
