# Pancakes

## Ingredients
### Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups of flour (210g)
- 3 tablespoons of sugar (48g)
- 2 teaspoons of baking powder (9g)
- 1 teaspoon of salt (5g)
- 1 teaspoon of baking soda (5g)
- 2 eggs (Room Temperature)
- 1 cup of milk/buttermilk (240ml)
- 2 tablespoons of melted butter (28g)
- 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract (5ml)

## Steps
1. mix dry
   whisk eggs, add milk (i used buttermilk), melted butter, + vanilla
   whisk
   add wet to dry, folding in with silicon spatula, do not overmix
   CLUMPS are good/okay do not overmix
   scoop out with icecream scoop onto preheated + buttered pan
   cook ~ 2-4 mins each side on low-med, med heat.
