# Cajun chicken

## Ingredients
- works for 2.5 lbs
- use salt to make tender
### marinade:
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 teaspoon herbs de province
- 1Tablespoon garlic
- 1 teaspoon cajun (walmart brand square)
### flour mix:
- 1.25 cup all purpose flour
- 4 Tablespoons panko
- 4 Tablespoons corn starch
- OR
- 1.75 cup rice flour
### flour seasoning:
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- pinch white pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 Tablespoon+ 1 teaspoon cajun
- 1/2 teaspoon parsley
