# Chicken and Dumplings

## Ingredients
- large condensed cream of chicken
- 14 chicken tenderloins
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup self-rising flour (1 cup all purpose flour, 1.5 tsp baking powder, 1/4 tsp salt)
- 1 cup ice water
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 5-6 carrots
- some radishes
- MSG
- 1/3 stick butter optional
- lima beans on side??/succotash?/corn & lima bean?

## Steps
1. Coat chicken in chicken bouillon while sitting out at room temp
   Place chicken in crock pot on high
   Add can of cream of chicken (large)
   Mix self-rising flour, all-purpose flour, cooking oil, and cold water (very sticky dough)
   Place on surface coated in extra flour and work flour into dough until handleable
   Roll out and cut with pizza cutter into dumplings
   Boil
   Add dumplings to chicken
   Using same water from dumplings, boil sliced/chopped carrots until soft
   Add carrots to crock pot
   Make a roux with flour, water, and stock from crock pot
   Add roux to crock pot once all lumps are gone and flour is cooked
   Add extra water to crock pot if needed
   Stir together and let sit on low for 30-45 minutes?
   Add MSG
   Add butter if you want
   nutritional yeast
   peas?
