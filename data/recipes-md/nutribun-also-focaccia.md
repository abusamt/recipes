# Nutribun also focaccia

## Ingredients
- 600g bread flour
- 84g dry milk
- 72g sugar
- 30g oil
- 15g salt
- 18g nutritional yeast
- 360+g water
- 5g rr yeast
- OR
- WITH poolish + autolysing
- 450g bread flour (FOR WHOLE WHEAT, REPLACE 200G OF 450G WITH WHOLE WHEAT)
- 84g dry milk
- 72g sugar
- 30g oil
- 18g nutritional yeast
- 300ish g/ml water or lessss
- later....
- 5g rr yeast
- 15g salt
- 150g poolish

## Steps
1. knead 10 minutes, rest 1-2 hours
   197g dough balls OR 98g
   rest 1-2hrs
   dust with 10g semolina mixed with 50g ap flour
   bake 350F (higher?)~15 minutes + steam
2. POOLISH + AUTOLYSE
   mix together bread flour, dry milk, sugar, nutritional yeast, oil, and water
   let rest 30 minutes
   then, add some water to dough (for salt and yeast)
   add salt and yeast
   combine with wet hand until yeast and salt evenly distributed throughout
   rest 30 minutes
   1st strength building fold
   rest 30 minutes
   2nd strength building fold
   Put in fridge overnight (at least 4 hours) if putting in fridge
   if not, let sit out 1-2 hours ish
