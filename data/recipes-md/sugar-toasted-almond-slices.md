# Sugar Toasted Almond Slices

## Ingredients
- 1 cups Almond slices
- 2 tablespoons Sugar (or more)
- 1 dash Salt
- 1 tbsp butter

## Steps
1. Med heat on induction burner
   Melt butter
   Add nuts & sugar & salt
   toast until desired toastiness
