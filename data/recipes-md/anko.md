# Anko

## Ingredients
- 400g bean
- 360g sugar
- half stick butter
- tablespoon of molasses
- tablespoon of corn syrup w/ vanilla
- teaspoon of salt
- teaspoon of pectin

## Steps
1. rinse beans or soak
   use good water for first "brew/boil"
   cover beans with good water in pot
   bring to boil, reduce heat for 10minutes (or until water turns tea coloured)
   strain off water for tea, add sugar
   add beans back into pot, cover with an inch of water
   bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 40 minutes- 2 hours until beans tender to when mashing
   strain beans through sieve with warmish water, let settle and then do again after pouring off excess liquid/water
