# chocolate glaze

for donuts etc

## Ingredients
- 1 TBS butter
- 2 TBS shortening
- 2 TBS cocoa
- 2 TBS hot water +
- 1/2 tsp vanillin sugar
- 1 tsp milk powder
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- for donuts add more powdered sugar + try corn syrup

## Steps
1. melt butter + shortening
   mix in rest

## Notes
- may have to heat up and whisk to get proper consistency
