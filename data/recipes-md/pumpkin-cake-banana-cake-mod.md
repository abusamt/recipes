# Pumpkin Cake (banana cake mod)

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup butter/shortening (half and half) (BROWN THE BUTTER, Set in freezer ~ 20min) 7TBS butter, 6 TBS shortening)
- 2.5 cups sugar
- some molasses
- 3 eggs
- 1.5 cups pureed pumpkin
- 3 cups sifted flour (half cake half all purpose)
- 3/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/3 cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- small dash/shake nutmeg
- 1-2 tsp cinnamon
- dash ground ginger
- vanilla optional (not used)
- For Frosting
- 1.5 box (cups?) (~ half a bag) (bag = 2lb or 907g) powdered sugar
- 3/4 cup (1.5 sticks) butter
- 3/4 cup pumpkin puree
- 1.5 tsp lemon juice
- 1/2 pkg cream cheese (4oz?)
### HALF Recipe:
- 4 TBS browned butter, 3 TBS shortening
- 1.25 Cup sugar
- molasses
- 1.5 Eggs
- 3/4 Cup pumpkin puree
- 1.5 Cups flour, sifted (3/4 C AP FL, 3/4 C cake flour)
- 3/8 tsp baking powder
- 2 TBS & 2tsp buttermilk
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- small dash (1/8 tsp?) nutmeg
- 1 ish tsp cinnamon
- dash (1/8tsp-1/4 tsp) ground ginger
- optional vanilla

## Steps
1. Cream together shortening, browned slightly firmed butter, sugar, & molasses blending well.
2. Add well beaten eggs and pumpkin, beat well
3. Sift flours then resift with dry ingredients (salt, b soda, b powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, salt)
4. Add dry alternatively with wet mixture of buttermilk combined
5. In greased, floured pans bake at 350F for about 5-7 minutes, then REDUCE temp to 330-345 for 5 minutes, then reduce more if needed for rest of cooking time (about 25-35 minutes)
6. Internal temp should be about 190F-200F (closer to 190F)
7. Let cool
### For Frosting:
8. Cream all ingredients together
9. Spread onto cooled cake or leave separate for individual slices
