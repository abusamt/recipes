# edible gingerbread

## Ingredients
- 9x13" pan with parchment
- oven temp: 350F
- 1 cup (237 mL) milk or water (used infused cream + butter) + added vanilla to that
- 1/2 cup (118 mL) molasses
- 1/2 cup (118 mL) honey
- 1/2 cup (one stick, 113g) butter (I used half shortening, half butter)
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 1/2 cups (300g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (maybe 3/4 if using unsalted butter)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda + PINCH OF BAKING POWDER
- 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
- 2 tablespoons ground ginger (use half if you don't want it very spicy)
- a little finely-ground black pepper

## Steps
1. Pour the cream into a pot on stove over low heat, add in shortening and butter as well as distillate
   warm and stir until all distillate is out
   Add splash of vanilla
   Remove from heat, add molasses and honey, stir, set aside
   Mix dry ingredients, flour, salt, b powder,+ b soda, spices-- whisk to combine to fluff flour
   Put the sugar in with the wet ingredients along with the egg and stir until smooth.
   Pour the wet mixture into the dry and whisk until just smooth
   make sure mix is slightly warm
   Pour into 9x13 or [bread loaf pan(takes longer)]
   Bake 15-35 mins
   Take out just before done
   Add marshmallows + broil
   Take out with parchment and cool on rack
