# chicken pinto burrito

## Ingredients
- 2 boneless skinless chicken thighs (or breast)
- 1 tbsp (14 grams) mayo
- Coarse kosher salt
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- Lime zest
- 1/2 juice lime
- Chile powder
- Mexican oregano (optional)
- 10 cranks of black pepper
- Pinto Beans
- Drizzle of oil
- Jalapeno, diced
- Sprinkle of whole cumin seeds
- 1 can pinto beans, drained
- Salt to taste
- Burrito Accoutrements
- Sour cream
- Thinly sliced lettuce
- Pickled onions
- Queso fresco
- Hot sauce
- Cilantro

## Steps
1. Set a cast-iron pan over medium heat. Mix the mayo and spices into a bowl. Add the chicken and slather the marinade on the exterior.
2. Add the chicken to the pan and brown on each side, about 3-4 minutes per side. Cook until the internal temp is 160. Slice and enjoy.
3. Drizzle oil into a pan over medium-low heat. Add diced jalapeno and cumin seeds and cook until just fragrant, about 1 minute. Add the pintos and cook, while stirring for another 4-5 minutes.
4. Spread a thin layer of sour cream on the bottom which will act as a glue for the beans and provide even coverage of the sour cream instead of getting large gloops everywhere.
5. For rolling purposes, I find it best to position the fillings in the upper third. First, hit it with a layer of the seasoned beans. Now here is where I like to add hot sauce as an acidic layer. Follow this with a generous helping of the chicken then a sprinkle of queso fresco, the diced tomatoes minced cilantro, some shredded lettuce, and you better not forget the pickled onions.
