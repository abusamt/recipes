# Almond Crescent Cookies

## Ingredients
- all purposes flour 2 Cups +2 tbs
- Roasted Crushed almond 1 Cup
- 180 g butter + shortening (90g each)
- powdered sugar 4 tbsp
- vanilla 1 tbsp
- baking powder 1tbsp
- 1 egg
- salt

## Steps
1. whisk vanillin sugar, flour, crushed almonds, baking powder, salt
   add in butter + shortening with hands
   add in egg with hands
   roll out and cut
   bake ~ 8-11 minutes
