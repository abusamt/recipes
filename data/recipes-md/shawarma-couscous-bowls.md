# Shawarma Couscous Bowls

## Ingredients
- 1/4 red onion, finely sliced
- 2.5 oz couscous
- 1oz dried apricot, diced small
- 1 Tbs butter or ghee
- 1 bell pepper or about 2-4 mini peppers, sliced
- ~9 oz or 4 carrots, cut at a bias
- 1 can or about 14 oz chickpeas
- small handful cilantro, chopped
- lemon juice
- 1 TBS sour cream
- 1 TBS yogurt
- harissa powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder or onion powder
- salt
- pinch sugar
- 2 tsp shawarma mix
- pepper
- bouillon
- 3/4c water

## Steps
1. Slice onion finely
   Soak onion in squeeze of lemon juice, pinch of salt and pinch of sugar
   Set aside
   Weigh out couscous, set aside
   Dice apricots, slice peppers, cut carrots at bias, rinse and dry chickpeas
   Prepare sauce-- 1TBS yogurt, 1TBS sour cream, squeeze lemon, harissa, some water (1tsp at atime), salt, pepper
   Toss chickpeas, carrots, and peppers in oil,
   Toss chickpeas and carrots in 1 tsp shawarma spice blend each
   Cook in oven at 375F ~ 20 minutes
   Cook in air fryer 350F ~ 20 minutes
   Melt butter/ghee low med heat
   When melted, add couscous and toss in butter until lightly browned (2-4 minutes)
   Once toasted, add some bouillon, 3/4 CUP water, and onion or garlic powder
   Bring to boil
   Once boiling, place heat on low/simmer, and cover
   Cook couscous until done (6-10 minutes)
   Once couscous is done, add diced apricot, stir
   Plate.
   Place couscous, then chickpeas and veggies, then sauce, cilantro and pickled onions
